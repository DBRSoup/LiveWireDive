# Live Wire Dive

When Live Wire is active, triggering a nose dive explosion also forcefully advances Live Wire to the next interval, causing it to explode with the dive and shortening its duration.

I was getting annoyed by Live Wire exploding while I'm at the top of my nose dive with Bruiser and wanted them to work better together :). 

As a consequence, the more you dive the less you'll have Live Wire active, as it advances its effect timer in order to trigger the explosion early.
